## Vision

A website that feels like a **high-end dental studio**: calm, premium, human and clinically professional. It must not look like a generic medical or hospital template.

Three words to design by: **Calm · Clear · Trustworthy**

## Business goals

1. Turn visitors into patients by making calling and appointment requests effortless.
2. Build trust through the doctor's approach, real photography and genuine reviews.
3. Explain treatments in plain language so patients feel informed, not scared.
4. Be found locally by people searching for a dentist in Palanpur and Surat.

## Visitor goals

| A visitor wants to… | The website answers with… |
|---|---|
| Know if this clinic is right for them | About page, doctor profile, care principles |

| Get there | Address, map, "Get Directions" link |
| Talk to someone now | Click-to-call everywhere, especially on mobile |

## Target audience (suggested personas, confirm with clinic)

| Persona | Need | Key pages |
|---|---|---|
| Local family | Regular check-ups and cleaning for the household | Home, General dentistry, Teeth cleaning, Patient guide |
| Anxious patient | Gentle, patient explanations and reassurance | About, Reviews, Patient guide |
| Person in pain | Fast help and a phone number | Dental emergency, Contact |

| Appearance-focused adult | Cosmetic and whitening options | Cosmetic dentistry, Teeth whitening |

## In scope (Phase 1)

- All 16 routes with unique, real layouts and content structure.
- Shared design system, components, header, footer and mobile navigation.
- Animations with reduced-motion support.
- Responsive layouts from 360px to 1440px+.
- SEO metadata, structured data (verified facts only), sitemap and robots.
- Appointment form UI, **clearly labeled as a demo**.
- README with setup and run instructions.

## Out of scope (Phase 1)

- Backend, database or API endpoints.
- Real appointment submission or online booking system.
- Payments, patient accounts or patient records.
- Storing any sensitive health information.
- Final legal text (privacy and terms need legal review).

- Number of appointment requests (once connected).
