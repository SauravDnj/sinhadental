## Never invent

If something is missing, leave it out. Never make up:

- Credentials, awards or years of experience.
- Prices or offers.
- Treatment counts or "patients served" numbers.
- Emergency availability.
- Insurance partnerships.
- Patient outcomes, testimonials or before/after photos.
