# 13 — Content Rules (Clinical, Review and Legal)

[← Back to README](../README.md)

> These rules protect patients and the clinic. They apply to every page, image and piece of metadata.

## Clinical content

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Explain treatments in plain, educational language | Diagnose visitors from symptoms |
| Say suitability is decided after an examination | Promise a treatment is right for someone |
| Describe benefits **and** limitations responsibly | Promise pain-free treatment |
| Use "may", "can", "often" where appropriate | Guarantee results or outcomes |
| Have the dentist review every clinical statement | State exact durations or session counts without approval |
| Encourage a professional evaluation | Show prices without clinic approval |

### Required disclaimer (treatment, guide and emergency pages)

> This information is general and educational. It isn't a diagnosis or personal medical advice. Please speak with the dentist about your situation.

### Urgent and emergency wording

| Situation | Website guidance |
|---|---|
| Severe tooth pain, broken tooth, lost filling | Call the clinic |
| Facial swelling, dental trauma, uncontrolled bleeding, fever | Seek urgent professional care |
| Difficulty breathing or swallowing | **Call emergency services immediately (112 in India)** |

Do not state emergency availability or after-hours care unless the clinic confirms it.

## Reviews and testimonials

- Use only genuine, public reviews, with permission and appropriate attribution.
- Keep excerpts faithful to their meaning; don't edit them to say more.
- Never invent names, dates, ratings or review text.
- Don't imply reviews guarantee any clinical outcome.
- Show the rating and count only after re-verification; record the check date.
- Always link to the live Google listing.

## Images

- No fake likeness of Dr. Sinha or patients.
- No unverified before/after images.
- Written consent for any identifiable person.
- Illustrative images must not suggest they show the clinic's own patients.

## Business facts

- Use only the values in [02-clinic-information.md](02-clinic-information.md).
- Show inclusion details (LGBTQ+ friendly, women-owned) only with confirmation.
- Unconfirmed services are removed or labeled "Ask the clinic."

## Appointment form honesty

- Phase 1: clearly labeled **"Demo form — no request is sent."**
- Never show "Appointment confirmed" unless a real, connected submission succeeded.
- Ask only for what's needed: name, phone, preferred time, optional short reason, consent.
- Never ask for detailed medical history, ID numbers or payment details.

## Legal pages

- Use clinic-approved, legally reviewed text only.
- Don't invent claims about data storage, cookies, tracking, security or retention.
- Placeholder legal text is clearly marked internal and never published as final.

## Writing style

- Warm, calm, plain English; short sentences and paragraphs.
- Address the reader as "you"; the clinic as "we".
- Avoid fear-based language and hype ("best", "No. 1", "painless", "guaranteed").
- Explain jargon once (e.g., "root canal treatment, which cleans the inside of a tooth").
