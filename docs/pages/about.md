## 1. Editorial hero

- **Eyebrow:** ABOUT THE STUDIO
- **H1:** Dental care built on calm, clear conversations.
- **Copy:** At Dr. Sinha's Dental Studio in Palanpur, Surat, we believe good dental care begins with understanding: your concerns, your questions and your comfort.
- Asymmetric layout: large image overlapping a mint panel; heading sits partly over whitespace.

## 2. Clinic philosophy

- **H2:** Why patients feel at ease here
- Three short paragraphs or a split layout:
  - **Comfort first:** a calm, welcoming environment.
  - **Clear explanations:** you understand what's happening and why.
  - **Informed decisions:** options explained, with no pressure.

## 3. Meet Dr. Sinha

- Approved portrait.
- Full name, verified qualifications and registration (as supplied).
- Approved biography (100–200 words).
- Areas of interest (only if supplied).
- **Never invent:** years of experience, awards, memberships or training.
- While details are missing, show a placeholder marked internally "Awaiting clinic biography" (not published).

## 4. Care principles

| Principle | One-line description |
|---|---|
| Listen first | We start by understanding what matters to you. |
| Explain clearly | Plain-language explanations at every step. |
| Respect your choices | You decide, with all the information you need. |
| Careful, thorough work | Attention to detail in every treatment. |

Cards reveal with a light stagger.

## 5. Clinic and team gallery

- 4–6 authentic images: entrance, reception, treatment room, equipment, team.
- Masonry or asymmetric grid; mobile swipe or 2-column grid.
- Descriptive alt text. Consent for identifiable people.

## 6. Patient journey

Short reuse of the "Your visit, made clearer" steps, or a simple line: Arrive → Talk → Understand → Decide.

## 7. Trust

- Verified rating and review count (if verified) + "Read reviews" link.
- Inclusion details (LGBTQ+ friendly, women-owned) **only if the clinic approves**, shown as a quiet line, not a badge wall.

## 8. CTA

- **H2:** Come and meet us
- Buttons: Request an Appointment · Get Directions

## SEO

- Title: About Dr. Sinha's Dental Studio · Palanpur, Surat
- Image alt text describes real people/places accurately.
