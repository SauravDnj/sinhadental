# Pages — Privacy Policy (`/privacy-policy`) and Terms (`/terms`)

[← Back to README](../../README.md)

**Goal:** clean, readable legal pages. **The text must be clinic-approved and legally reviewed.**

## Shared layout

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| H1 | "Privacy Policy" / "Website Terms of Use" |
| Last updated | Date line under the H1 |
| Table of contents | Jump links (sticky sidebar on desktop, top list on mobile) |
| Body | Readable width (~70ch), H2 per section, generous spacing |
| Contact | How to contact the clinic about the policy |
| Breadcrumbs | Home › Privacy Policy |

## Privacy policy: sections to be written by the clinic / legal reviewer

1. Who we are
2. What information the website collects
3. How the appointment form handles information (Phase 1: demo, nothing sent)
4. Cookies and analytics (only if actually used)
5. How information is used and shared
6. How long information is kept
7. Your rights and choices
8. Contact us
9. Changes to this policy

## Terms: sections to be written by the clinic / legal reviewer

1. About this website
2. Information is general, not medical advice
3. Appointment requests are not confirmed bookings
4. Intellectual property
5. External links (e.g., Google Maps)
6. Limitation of liability
7. Governing law
8. Contact

## Rules

- Do not invent claims about data storage, cookies, tracking, security or retention.
- Until reviewed, the pages show a clearly marked **internal placeholder** and must not be launched as final.
- Update the privacy policy before connecting the form or adding analytics.
