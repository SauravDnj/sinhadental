## 3. Before your first visit

Checklist style (clinic to confirm):

- Arrive a few minutes early.
- Bring any previous dental records or X-rays, if you have them.
- Bring a list of medicines you take.
- Note down any questions or concerns.
- Tell us if you feel nervous; we'll take it slowly.

## 4. During your consultation

Steps (clinic to confirm):

1. A conversation about your concerns.
2. An examination.
3. A clear explanation of what the dentist sees.
4. Options discussed; you decide what's next.

## 5. Asking questions

Encouraging copy plus example questions:

- What are my options?
- What happens if I wait?
- What should I expect after treatment?

## 6. Everyday oral care tips

General tips, reviewed by the dentist (e.g., brushing twice a day, flossing, regular check-ups). Presented as simple icon cards.
