# Page — Patient Reviews (`/patient-reviews`)

[← Back to README](../../README.md)

**Goal:** build trust with genuine patient voices, and send visitors to the live Google listing.
**Primary CTA:** Read all reviews on Google · **Secondary:** Request an Appointment

## Section order

| # | Section |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hero with verified rating summary |
| 2 | What patients often mention (themes) |
| 3 | Selected review excerpts |
| 4 | Link to the full Google listing |
| 5 | CTA |

---

## 1. Hero

- **Eyebrow:** PATIENT REVIEWS
- **H1:** What patients say about their visits
- Rating summary card: "5.0 on Google · 70 reviews" (**only after re-verification**), with a single small star, not a row of big stars.
- Internal record: date the rating was last verified (kept in [02-clinic-information.md](../02-clinic-information.md), not necessarily shown).

## 2. What patients often mention

Short, honest summary of themes, presented as icons + labels:

- Calm and polite doctor
- Clear explanations
- Empathetic care
- Help during a dental emergency
- Thorough treatment

Wording: "Patients often mention…" (themes, not invented quotes).

## 3. Selected review excerpts

- 3–6 ReviewCards with genuine, approved excerpts.
- Source label "Google review", date if available, first name or initial only if public and appropriate.
- Excerpts kept faithful to their meaning.
- Layout: calm 2–3 column grid or keyboard-accessible carousel (no auto-advance).

## 4. Full listing

- Card: "See all our reviews, and leave your own, on Google." → Google Maps link (opens externally, labeled).

## 5. CTA

Standard CTA band.

## Rules

- No fabricated names, dates, ratings or text.
- No review structured data.
- Small note: "Reviews reflect individual experiences; treatment outcomes vary."
