## Template rules

- No pain-free promises, guaranteed results, exact durations, prices or suitability claims.
- No symptom-based diagnosis. Encourage a professional evaluation.
- Unconfirmed service → remove page, or keep with an "Ask the clinic" label.
- Each page has its own title, meta description, H1 and image.

## 1. General dentistry — `/treatments/general-dentistry`

- **H1:** General Dentistry in Palanpur, Surat
- **Summary:** Everyday dental care, including check-ups, consultations and fillings, to help keep your teeth and gums healthy.
- **When might someone ask:** time for a routine check-up · a tooth feels sensitive · a filling feels rough or has come out · general questions about oral health.
- **What to expect:** 1) Talk about your concerns → 2) Examination → 3) Options explained → 4) Treatment or next visit planned.
- **FAQ ideas:** How often should I have a check-up? · What happens at a check-up? · Do you do fillings?
- **Related:** Teeth cleaning, Root canal, Dental emergency.

## 2. Teeth cleaning — `/treatments/teeth-cleaning`

- **H1:** Professional Teeth Cleaning
- **Summary:** A professional clean removes build-up that brushing at home can miss, and is a good time to check gum health.
- **When might someone ask:** due for a routine clean · gums bleed when brushing · visible build-up · fresh start for oral hygiene.
- **What to expect:** 1) Gum and teeth check → 2) Cleaning → 3) Polishing → 4) Home-care tips.
- **Aftercare ideas:** brushing and flossing tips, when to return (dentist to advise).
- **FAQ ideas:** How often should I get my teeth cleaned? · Is cleaning uncomfortable? *(answer carefully, no promises)*
- **Related:** General dentistry, Teeth whitening.

## 3. Root canal — `/treatments/root-canal`

- **H1:** Root Canal Treatment
- **Summary:** Root canal treatment cleans the inside of a tooth when the inner pulp is infected or inflamed, and may help save the natural tooth.
- **When might someone ask:** lingering tooth pain · sensitivity to hot or cold that stays · a dentist has recommended it · a deep cavity or cracked tooth.
- **What to expect:** 1) Examination and X-ray → 2) Options explained → 3) Treatment → 4) Restoring the tooth (e.g., crown, if advised).
- **Benefits / limitations:** may preserve the natural tooth; number of visits and outcomes vary and are discussed with the dentist.
- **FAQ ideas:** Will I need a crown? · How many visits? *(clinic to answer)*
- **Urgent note:** facial swelling or fever → seek urgent care; breathing/swallowing difficulty → call 112.
- **Related:** General dentistry, Dental emergency.

## 4. Braces — `/treatments/braces`

- **H1:** Braces and Orthodontic Consultation
- **Summary:** Braces can gradually move teeth into a better position. A consultation helps you understand whether they may suit you and what the process involves.
- **When might someone ask:** crowded or gapped teeth · bite concerns · a parent asking for a child or teen · adults considering straightening.
- **What to expect:** 1) Consultation → 2) Assessment and records → 3) Plan explained → 4) Regular adjustment visits.
- **FAQ ideas:** What age can braces start? · How long does treatment take? *(answer only with clinic-approved, general wording)* · What types of braces are offered? *(confirm)*
- **Related:** Cosmetic dentistry, General dentistry.

## 5. Cosmetic dentistry — `/treatments/cosmetic-dentistry`

- **H1:** Cosmetic Dentistry
- **Summary:** Cosmetic dentistry focuses on the appearance of your smile. Options are discussed in a consultation based on your teeth and goals.
- **When might someone ask:** discoloured, chipped or uneven teeth · gaps · wanting a more confident smile.
- **What to expect:** 1) Talk about your goals → 2) Examination → 3) Options and limitations explained → 4) Plan agreed.
- **Rules:** list specific procedures only if confirmed. No before/after images unless genuine, consented and approved. No "perfect smile" promises.
- **Related:** Teeth whitening, Braces.

## 6. Teeth whitening — `/treatments/teeth-whitening`

- **H1:** Teeth Whitening Consultation
- **Summary:** Whitening may lighten natural tooth colour. A consultation checks whether it's suitable and what results may be realistic.
- **When might someone ask:** stained or yellowed teeth · preparing for an event · wanting a brighter smile.
- **What to expect:** 1) Check teeth and gums → 2) Discuss suitability and expectations → 3) Whitening plan → 4) Aftercare.
- **Limitations:** results vary; fillings and crowns don't whiten; sensitivity may occur (dentist to word).
- **Imagery:** natural smiles only; no exaggerated white results.
- **Related:** Cosmetic dentistry, Teeth cleaning.
